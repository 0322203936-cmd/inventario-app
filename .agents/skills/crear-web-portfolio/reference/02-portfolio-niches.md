# Portfolio Niches — the field decides everything

The single most important decision: **what does this person do?** The niche
picks the core section (how the work is shown), the archetype family, and the
tone. Everything else follows. Below: the niches, each with its core mechanic,
recommended archetype, and what "great" looks like.

The niche is a starting point, not a cage — a person can be two things
(illustrator + musician), in which case pick the dominant one for the archetype
and give the second its own section.

---

## 1. Visual artist / illustrator / designer — `arte`

**Core section: the gallery.** The art is enormous; the UI disappears.

- Masonry or justified grid of high-res pieces, click → lightbox with title,
  year, medium, and "comisióname". Filter by series/medium.
- Optional: a featured hero piece full-bleed; a "series" view grouping works.
- Archetype: **Editorial Light Cream** or **Brutalist Grid** (let the work
  carry the color) — never a loud gradient that fights the art.
- Great = the images are crisp, big, correctly proportioned, fast; the person's
  style is obvious in 3 seconds. Chrome is quiet.
- CTA: commission / buy prints / hire for projects.

## 2. Developer / web designer / product maker — `dev`

**Core section: case studies.** Not a grid of logos — a few deep stories.

- Per project: hero shot/mockup, the problem, what they built, the stack (as
  quiet tags), a result or metric, and a live link + repo. 3-6 strong cases beat
  20 thumbnails.
- A compact "stack" strip and a services list ("webs a medida", "landing +
  SEO").
- Archetype: **Glassmorphism Modern**, **Mouse-Reactive Gradient** or
  **Cinematic 3D** — tech-forward but legible.
- Great = a hiring manager reads one case and gets it; live demos work; code is
  reachable.
- CTA: hire / contact / view live projects.

## 3. Musician / producer / composer — `musica`

**Core section: the player.** You can hear the work without leaving.

- Track list with inline play (HTML5 `<audio>`, no external SDK), waveform or a
  tasteful equalizer, cover art, and links to Spotify/Apple/YouTube. Featured
  track up top.
- Optional: "para sync/licensing" section (ads, film), gigs list.
- Archetype: **Editorial Dark Warm** or **Cinematic 3D** — atmospheric, moody.
- Great = the visitor presses play in the first screen; audio is smooth; the
  vibe matches the music.
- CTA: license a track / book / follow on streaming.

## 4. Photographer / videographer / video editor — `foto`

**Core section: full-bleed series.** Immersive, edge-to-edge.

- Horizontal or scroll-driven series, minimal text, big imagery, per-series
  intro. For a video editor / filmmaker: a **reel** (poster + lightweight embed,
  no autoplay walls), before/after or shot breakdowns, client logos. A
  "latest work" carousel can auto-pull from their channel (`16-creator-live-modules.md`).
- Archetype: **Editorial Dark Warm** (photos) or **Magazine Multi-Page** (many
  series).
- Great = images fill the screen, load fast, and the eye never fights the UI.
- CTA: book a shoot / license / contact.

## 5. Writer / journalist / copywriter — `escritura`

**Core section: selected clips.** Words, framed with typographic care.

- List of pieces (title, outlet, year, one-line, link/PDF), grouped by type
  (features, essays, copy). Pull-quotes as design elements. Reading-first.
- Archetype: **Newspaper Editorial** or **Editorial Light Cream**.
- Great = the typography is impeccable, the best clip is first, everything is one
  click to read.
- CTA: hire for writing / commission / contact.

## 6. Personal brand / creator / professional trajectory — `trayectoria`

**Core section: the story + milestones.** Who they are, what they've built, the
proof. (This is the Adrián-Sáenz-style portfolio: entrepreneur, investor,
creator, speaker.)

- Hero with name + roles + one flagship stat; a stats band (subscribers,
  views, revenue, years); an origin/story section; a "what I do today" trio; a
  **milestones timeline** (talks, features, achievements); press logos; a
  manifesto/values block; collaborations; contact split by intent.
- Archetype: **Editorial Dark Warm** or **Cinematic 3D** — authoritative,
  premium, personal.
- Great = credibility is immediate (numbers + press), the story is human, and
  there's a clear way to reach them for each kind of deal.
- CTA: business / press / investment — often split by intent.
- **Optional live proof** (only if real & public): a rolling scoreboard counter,
  a real live subscriber/follower count, a platform-search reveal animation, and
  an auto-updating "latest content" carousel — all in `16-creator-live-modules.md`.

## 7. Multi-discipline / generalist — `multi`

**Core section: a curated "what I do" hub** that routes to sub-sections.

- A hub home with 2-4 disciplines, each linking to its own section built with
  the matching niche's mechanic (art → gallery, dev → cases). One archetype
  across all for coherence.
- Archetype: **Magazine Multi-Page** or **Brutalist Grid**.
- Great = one identity, several bodies of work, no confusion about who they are.
- CTA: the primary discipline's CTA, plus a general contact.

## Niche → archetype quick map

| Niche | First archetype | Alt | Core section |
|---|---|---|---|
| `arte` | Editorial Light Cream | Brutalist Grid | Gallery + lightbox |
| `dev` | Glassmorphism Modern | Mouse-Reactive Gradient / Cinematic 3D | Case studies |
| `musica` | Editorial Dark Warm | Cinematic 3D | Audio player |
| `foto` | Editorial Dark Warm | Magazine Multi-Page | Full-bleed series |
| `escritura` | Newspaper Editorial | Editorial Light Cream | Selected clips |
| `trayectoria` | Editorial Dark Warm | Cinematic 3D | Story + timeline |
| `multi` | Magazine Multi-Page | Brutalist Grid | Discipline hub |

Full archetype recipes (palette, type, effects, hero) live in the inherited
archetype rules and `03-effects-catalog.md`. **Pick ONE archetype per site**,
rotate across builds so no two portfolios feel alike.

## Choosing / advising (🎯 Direction)

- If the person named their field, just build it.
- If unsure ("quiero una web para encontrar trabajo"), ask what they'd show:
  images → `arte`/`foto`; projects → `dev`; audio → `musica`; writing →
  `escritura`; a career → `trayectoria`.
- Push for **fewer, stronger pieces** over a big thin grid — a portfolio is
  judged by its best item and its worst, not its count.
- The purpose (get hired vs. offer services vs. sell work) tunes the CTA and the
  services section, not the niche.
